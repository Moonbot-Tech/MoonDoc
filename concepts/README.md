# Chapters: how trading in Moonbot works

The eight chapters are the semantic layer of the knowledge base: what happens on the market, which terminal mechanism answers it, which parameters control it and where its traps are. The chapters can be read in order or by task; the exact parameter tables are in [`reference/`](../reference/README.md), ready-made templates in [`examples/strategies/`](../examples/strategies/README.md).

| Chapter | What it is about | Start here if |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [**01. Coin selection for scalping and the market filter system**](01-market-selection-and-filters.md) | The Coins table, liquidity, deltas, `BTC` and market context, dangerous states, how filters live inside a trade, penalties, "Filters on charts" | the strategy is silent or trades the wrong coins |
| [**02. Finding trading situations: detects and external signals**](02-detect-pipeline-and-signals.md) | What a detect is, a map of all strategy types, detects by trade feed and order book, liquidations, Watcher, Telegram and news, several strategies on one coin, the Autotrading module and Free/PRO | you are choosing a strategy type or do not understand why a signal did not become an order |
| [**03. Spike hunting: MoonShot, MoonHook, manual hooks, MoonStrike and SellShot**](03-spike-hunting-mechanics.md) | The three phases of a spike and three strategies for them, the `MoonShot` corridor and distance adjustments, the `MoonHook` pullback and order, entering into the strike with `MoonStrike`, the runaway take-profit, futures trading basics | you trade spikes |
| [**04. Strategy links: Master → Slave triggers, sessions and joining take-profits**](04-triggers-and-master-slave.md) | Keys and their life, permission / prohibition / emergency exit, clearing keys, scout and sniper, the shield against a `BTC` dump, coin and strategy sessions, `JoinSellKey` | you are assembling a link of several strategies |
| [**05. CustomEMA formulas: the state of a coin and the market through moving averages, extremes, volumes and prices on other exchanges**](05-ema-geometry-and-formulas.md) | Two points in time, three scales, `Avg`, `MAX`/`MIN`, `BTC` and `MAvg` context, volume, arbitrage, connectives and parser errors, where the formula lives in a trade, telemetry | you are writing a condition that is not among the filters |
| [**06. Orders, take-profit and stops: how the terminal manages a trade**](06-orders-exits-and-stops-cascade.md) | Entry and pending orders, grid and merging, four take-profit tracking algorithms, stops and trailing, liquidation protection, three `Panic Sell` modes, percentages in a short | you are working out why a trade closed the way it did |
| [**07. Moon Kernel, a trading VPS, the control bot and working with exchanges**](07-kernel-vps-and-infrastructure.md) | Kernel and client, ping, the server under load, the Problems window, Linux, the control bot, exchange limits, Hyperliquid | you are putting the bot on a server or protecting positions from a VPS failure |
| [**08. Testing and debugging strategies: "Filters on charts", the Pencil, the Emulator, BackTest and reports**](08-testing-debugging-and-analytics.md) | What is checked with what, drawing spikes, the emulator and backtest with their limits, reports and the total percentage, the Problems window, the "why the strategy does not buy" checklist | you are checking a new strategy or looking for why it does not trade |

The chapters' statements are verified against the terminal's source code, the parameter tooltips and the [moonbot.pro](https://moonbot.pro/documentation) documentation; numbers are given with their source.
