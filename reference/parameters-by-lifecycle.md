## 2. Matrix of key interrelations across the 5 trade phases

Below are the most important cross-tab dependencies: what a parameter needs in order to work (`Requires`), which settings it overrides (`Overrides`) and what disables it (`Disabled by`).

### Phase 1. Market selection and preliminary filtering (before the signal)

| Parameter | Tab | Purpose and mechanics | Requires | Overrides | Disabled by / specifics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`IgnoreFilters`** | `Filters` | Skips the check of 6 filter subgroups (`Base`, `Time`, `Price`, `Ping`, `Volume`, `Delta`) | — | Disables `CustomEMA` in all strategies **except `SignalType=EMA`** (where the detector checks the formula itself) | **Does not disable:** `Session` (needs `IgnoreSession=YES`), `OnlyNewListing`, `DontTradeListing`, `LeveragedTokens`, `ListedType`, `CoinsWhiteList/BlackList`, `Triggers`, `SellByCustomEMA` |
| **`IgnoreSession`** | `Session` | Disables blocking of the strategy by the `SessionPenaltyTime` pause and order scaling by session | — | Overrides the session stop | Does not reset the accumulated session counter (only ignores blocking by it) |
| **`CustomEMA`** | `Filters / Base` | Checks a logical formula of the functions `EMA`, `Avg`, `MAX`, `MIN`, `Vol`, `BTC`, `MAvg`, `Arb` on detect and before the buy (in `MoonShot` — also while the order stands) | `AND` / `OR` operators (no grouping parentheses); only a numeric constant to the right of `>` / `<` | At a threshold `<= -887` for `>` (`> -888`) or `>= 887` for `<` (`< 888`) and `Vol > 0` the comparison is skipped and the values are written to the report (data is still required) — collector mode | Disabled with **`IgnoreFilters=YES`** or **`IgnoreBase=YES`** (but in `SignalType=EMA` it is checked by the detector itself); requires the Autotrading module |
| **`BinanceTokenTags`** | `Filters / Base` | Filters exchange tags (`!Monitoring`, `!Alpha`, `New`, `Seed`, `!OICapped`, `TradFi`) | The tag service enabled; auto-update at `:00` and `:02` of every hour | `Monitoring` lists are applied cross-exchange, including on Bybit/Gate | Disabled with `IgnoreFilters=YES` or `IgnoreBase=YES` |
| **`MaxPing`** | `Filters / Ping` | Limits the maximum allowed network ping to the exchange | **Requires `MaxLatency > 0`** | Widened by `+10%` while an order is held | **Not checked when `MaxLatency = 0`** (while `MinPing` works independently) |
| **`MaxLatency`** | `Filters / Ping` | Blocks entry when the trade/order stream latency is exceeded and sets a 7-second pause at the strategy level | — | Activates the `MaxPing` check | Disabled with `IgnoreFilters=YES` or `IgnorePing=YES` |
| **`MarkPriceMin` / `Max`** | `Filters / Base` | Forbids entry on a dangerous deviation of `MarkPrice` from the current futures price (taking the `Long`/`Short` direction into account) | Futures market; **value `!= 0`** | Re-checked on the signal path once a bidirectional detector has determined the trade side | Belongs to the **`Base`** subgroup (disabled with **`IgnoreBase=YES`**, not `IgnorePrice`). **A value of `0` fully disables the corresponding bound** |
| **`FundingBefore` / `After`** | `Filters / Time` | Sets a working window (`> 0`) or a pause (`< 0`) for `X` s before and `Y` s after the funding clearing | Futures market only | — | On a spot market the funding check is skipped. Disabled with `IgnoreFilters=YES` or `IgnoreTime=YES` |
| **`Delta_3h_Max` / `Delta_24h_Max`** | `Filters / Delta` | Check the maximum of the corresponding delta and the hourly delta: **`Max(Delta_3h, Delta_1h)`** and **`Max(Delta_24h, Delta_1h)`** | — | React instantly to a fresh hourly impulse | When the line is omitted, `Delta_3h_Max` takes the default **`20%`** (and writing `0` blocks all pairs with a non-zero delta) |

### Phase 2. Detect and entry price calculation (`MoonShot`, `MoonHook`, `Spread`, `Drops`)

| Parameter | Tab | Purpose and mechanics | Requires | Overrides | Disabled by / specifics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`MShotPriceMin` / `MShotPrice`** | `MoonShot` | `MShotPrice` is the distance from the reference price at which the order stands; `MShotPriceMin` is the near corridor boundary: when the price comes closer, the order is moved back to `MShotPrice`. The far boundary is built automatically (with the default `7/2` the corridor is `2…9%`) | `SignalType = MoonShot` | The terminal has no minimum corridor width — you set it | Normalized on save (`MShotPriceMin >= 0.001`, `MShotPrice >= MShotPriceMin + 0.001`); with `MShotPriceMin > MShotPrice` the corridor degenerates into a line |
| **`MShotAddDistance`** | `MoonShot` | Widens the corridor in proportion to the sum of deltas: the order (and with it the far boundary) is shifted by `AddDelta * (1 + MShotAddDistance / 100)` | Non-zero `MShotAdd*Delta` modifiers | Protects against exhausting API limits at high volatility | All terms are non-negative and only deepen the order (except `MShotAddMarkDelta`, which depends on the side); `MShotMinusSatoshi=YES` merely keeps the limit order from sitting right against the reference price |
| **`FastShotAlgo`** | `MoonShot` | Fast mode (enabled by default): with a non-zero `MShotRaiseWait`, the "current" trade is the deepest trade of the last instant rather than the last one in the batch, and the take-profit is placed immediately on the fill. Also acts on `MoonHook` when `HookRaiseWait > 0` | `SignalType = MoonShot` (or `MoonHook`) | An instant take-profit may close the trade inside the same spike if the take-profit is close to the entry | Re-placement pauses are set by `MShotReplaceDelay`, not by unticking the checkbox; against a take-profit close in the same spike — a small `SellDelay` |
| **`HookTimeFrame`** | `MoonHook` | The spike search window in seconds, which at the same time sets the **repeat-trigger pause** on this coin | `SignalType = MoonHook` | Also sets the turnover estimation window for `BuyOrderReduce` | Automatically clamped to **`0.4 .. 10.0` s** |
| **`HookInterpolate` / `HookInitialPrice`** | `MoonHook` | Determine the level of the **first order placement** at the moment of detect by one of 5 modes (`0..4`) | `SignalType = MoonHook` | In modes `0`/`1` with `HookPriceDistance > 0` (default `25`) the first order does not leave the hook corridor: no closer than `(100 − HookPriceDistance)%` and no farther than `(100 + HookPriceDistance)%` of the spike depth from the price; the order lands exactly at `HookInitialPrice` only with `HookPriceDistance = 0` or a wide corridor | Afterwards the order is managed within the `HookPriceDistance` corridor; in mode `2` the corridor is fitted to the order |
| **`DetectModifier`** | `Delta Modifiers` | Raises the detect trigger threshold by `sum_delta * DetectModifier` | Non-zero `Add*Delta` coefficients | Shifts the lower threshold of `HookDetectDepth` / `Drops` / `Pump` | In `MoonHook` **does not shift the upper limit `HookDetectDepthMax`** — if the lower threshold exceeds the upper one, the detect stops |
| **`BuyOrderReduce` / `MinReducedSize`** | `MoonHook` / `Spread` | Estimates the allowed order volume in proportion to the trade-feed turnover within the detect window (`TimeInterval` in `Spread`, `HookTimeFrame` in `MoonHook`) | `BuyOrderReduce > 0` | In both strategies cuts `OrderSize` down to the estimated volume (not below the exchange minimum) and rejects the signal when the estimate is `< MinReducedSize`; a manual hook is not reduced | With `BuyOrderReduce = 0` the `MinReducedSize` parameter is not applied |

### Phase 3. Order placement, `MultiOrders` grids and entry triggers

| Parameter | Tab | Purpose and mechanics | Requires | Overrides | Disabled by / specifics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`BuyType`** (`Buy`, `BuyStop`, `BuyLimit`) | `Buy` | Chooses between a real limit order in the order book (`Buy`) and a virtual pending order in the terminal's memory (`BuyStop`/`BuyLimit`) protected from single ticks | For `BuyStop`/`BuyLimit`, `PendingOrderSpread` is used | After the conditional line is crossed, places a real limit order for **3 seconds** | The base value **`AutoCancelBuy = 300`** cancels virtual orders after 5 minutes (to hold them for a long time, set `AutoCancelBuy = 0`) |
| **`CheckAfterBuy`** | `Filters` | Continuously checks the filters (with a `±DeltaSwitch` hysteresis) while the opening `Buy` order stands in the order book | In `MoonShot` always forced on; in other strategies — by the flag | — | A one-time check at the moment the `Buy` order is placed is always performed even with `CheckAfterBuy=NO` (disabled only by the parameter **`DontCheckBeforeBuy=YES`**) |
| **`HFT`** | `Buy` | Passes the exchange a request validity window `recvWindow = HFT` (ms) on opening `Buy` orders (and on the Bybit WS API — also on selling and `Amend` modification) | — | The exchange automatically rejects an order delayed by network lag without incrementing the API error counter | The window is counted from a timestamp that the terminal itself aligns with the exchange clock; a rejection by `HFT` is a normal event on a ping jump ([chapter 07](../concepts/07-kernel-vps-and-infrastructure.md)) |
| **`JoinSellKey` / `JoinPriceFixed`** | `MultiOrders` | Joins the `Sell` orders of several entries for a common exit. With `JoinPriceFixed=NO` the target is the arithmetic mean of the current percentages of the merged `Sell` orders (not weighted by size) from the new average entry price, with `YES` — the positive computed `SellPrice` percentage of the owner strategy (not applied to `MoonHook` and `MoonStrike`); neither the monetary amount nor the first order's percentage is preserved | Real trading (`EmulatorMode=NO`) | Assigns the merged order **the first running strategy in the list (started with the Start button) with this `JoinSellKey`, and if none is running — the first ticked one** | If the working strategy is disabled, the settings may go to another ticked strategy with the same key |
| **`ActiveTrigger`** | `Triggers` | Extends the key the whole time a buy is awaited — both while a real `Buy` order stands in the order book and while the strategy waits for a virtual `BuyStop` / `BuyLimit` to be crossed | A non-zero trigger key | In **`MoonShot`** holds **`TriggerKeyBuy` even before the purchase**, in other strategies — `TriggerKey`; slave strategies are allowed to enter before the master's order reaches the exchange | After the buy fills or is cancelled, the extension stops; slaves run out their `TriggerSeconds` |
| **`CancelByTriggerBL`** | `Triggers` | Instantly cancels standing `Buy` orders when a key from `TriggerKeysBL` is activated | A key in `TriggerKeysBL` | Fires **even with `CheckAfterBuy = NO`** (in `MoonShot` a key from `TriggerKeysBL` always cancels the `Buy`) | — |
| **`SessionPlusCount` / `SessionMinusCount`** | `Session` | Enable session progression: starting from which consecutive positive (negative) session `OrderSize` grows (shrinks) and the `SessionStratMax` / `SessionStratMin` thresholds change | Autotrading module (or the global emulation mode); non-zero `SessionIncreaseOrder` / `SessionReduceOrder` / `SessionStrat*` | — | **`0` (default) disables the progression**, it does not mean "from the first session": with the default counters, `SessionIncreaseOrder`, `SessionReduceOrder`, `SessionStratIncreaseMax`, `SessionStratReduceMin` silently do not work. For the progression to start from the first session, set `1`; `IgnoreSession = YES` disables it too |

### Phase 4. Holding the position and take-profit tracking algorithms

| Parameter | Tab | Purpose and mechanics | Requires | Overrides | Disabled by / specifics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`SellByTriggerBL`** | `Triggers` | Starts an emergency position close (`Panic Sell`) when the specified blacklist key is activated | **Nested inside the `TriggerKeysBL` check** | Has the highest emergency-exit priority | If the key is not also listed in `TriggerKeysBL`, the `SellByTriggerBL` check **will not run at all** |
| **`SellByCustomEMA`** | `Sell` / `Base` | Closes the position the same way as the exit by filters (with `SellByFiltersDelay` — first moving the take-profit, then `Panic Sell`) when the formula becomes true; the collector threshold `> -888` with `SellEMACheckEnter=YES` forbids buying wherever the formula has data | After `SellEMADelay` s from the placement of the `Sell` order | Works even with `IgnoreFilters=YES`; on closing the trade, arms `TriggerKeyProfit` (if `profit > 0`) or `TriggerKeyLoss` | The condition `>-888` inside `SellByCustomEMA` causes an immediate close of the position right after the purchase |
| **`SellByFilters` / `SellByFiltersDelay`** | `Sell` | Closes the position when the market leaves the strategy's filter bounds. With `SellByFiltersDelay >= 0.1` works in **2 phases**: first moves the limit take-profit to the current price (down to `PriceDownAllowedDrop`), then after `Delay` turns on `Panic Sell` | `SellByFilters > 0` (seconds after the `Sell` order is placed) | — | Does not check the `CustomEMA` entry formula (the exit by formula is served by `SellByCustomEMA`) |
| **`SellShot`** | `Sell` | Tracks the `Sell` order in a dynamic corridor above the current price on the `MoonShot` principle | `IgnoreSellShot = NO`, after `SellShotDelay` s from the placement of the `Sell` order | Allows holding the position through the whole impulse rise | Overridden by any stop loss or `Panic Sell` firing |
| **`SellSpread`** | `Sell` | Holds the `Sell` order within the price range of the last seconds, at an offset of `SellSpreadDistance` from its far boundary, not below `SellSpreadAllowedDrop` | `IgnoreSellSpread = NO`, after `SellSpreadDelay` s from the placement of the `Sell` order | — | Overridden by stop losses; `SellSpreadDistance` is clamped to `[-100 .. -1]` |
| **`SellLevel`** (`Delay`, `Time`, `Adjust`, `Count`, `WorkTime`) | `Sell` | Moves the `Sell` order to the maximum of trades over `SellLevelTime` + `SellLevelAdjust %`, and if the computed level is below `SellLevelAllowedDrop` — postpones the move | `SellLevelTime > 0`, `SellLevelCount > 0`, `SellLevelDelay > 0`; `SellLevelWorkTime = 0` (no limit) or greater than `SellLevelDelay` (`SellLevelWorkTime = 1` effectively disables the algorithm) | A manual take-profit move does **not** stop `SellLevel` (it stops only `PriceDown`); `SellLevel` moves the order over the manual level until `SellLevelWorkTime` or `SellLevelCount` run out | Does not reset the `PriceDown` timer: both algorithms work at the same time and can move the take-profit toward each other — separate them in time or keep one |
| **`PriceDown`** (`Delay`, `Timer`, `Percent`, `Relative`, `AllowedDrop`) | `Sell` | After `PriceDownTimer` s from the placement of the `Sell` order, every `PriceDownDelay` s lowers the take-profit price toward the market. Accumulates fractional steps `< 1 tick` up to a whole price step | `PriceDownTimer > 0` | Stops for the order as soon as someone else has moved the take-profit — the trader with the mouse or the `SellShot` / `SellSpread` algorithms (`SellLevel` moves do not stop it) | On **Short** blocked when `PriceDownAllowedDrop <= -100` |

### Phase 5. Stop-loss cascade, trailing and emergency close (`Panic Sell`)

| Parameter | Tab | Purpose and mechanics | Requires | Overrides | Disabled by / specifics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`UseBV_SV_Stop` / `BV_SV_Reverse`** | `Stops` | Closes the position by the buy-to-sell volume ratio `BV/SV` (starts working almost immediately after the take-profit is placed, **regardless of `StopLossDelay`**). For a long the fraction is buys to sells, for a short — sells to buys; `BV_SV_Reverse=YES` swaps the numerator and denominator, the threshold stays the same, so it is set as the reciprocal (exit from a long when buys are twice the sells — `BV_SV_Ratio = 0.5`) | The smoothed bid and the last trade **on the favorable side** of the `BV_SV_TakeProfit` threshold (for a long — above, for a short — below); the condition is re-checked every time | With `BV_SV_TakeProfit < 0.1` also works as an entry filter before the buy | Once the price returns past the threshold the stop is inactive, there is no latch (with a positive `BV_SV_TakeProfit = +1%` it works as protection of profit already accumulated `> +1%`). The closing step is `TrailingSpread` |
| **`StopLossDelay`** | `Stops` | A delay (supports fractional seconds) from the moment the `Sell` order is placed before `Stop 1/2/3` and `Trailing` are allowed to activate | `UseStopLoss = YES` | Blocks the firing of stop losses and trailing in the first seconds of position tracking | The `TimeToSwitch2Stop` and `TimeToSwitchStop3` timers **count in parallel** from the same moment the `Sell` order is placed |
| **`TimeToSwitch2Stop` & `PriceToSwitch2Stop`** | `Stops` | Move the main stop to the `SecondStopLoss` level strictly by a logical **`AND`** and only with `UseStopLoss` enabled | Both conditions met simultaneously (`Time` and `Price`) | If it fires later than the third stop (`Stop 3`), **it overwrites the `Stop 3` level with its own value** | With `PriceToSwitch2Stop = 0` waits for the price on the favorable side of the entry (long — above, short — below the mirrored level) and will not switch in a drawdown — set a negative threshold |
| **`FastStopLoss`** | `Stops` | Accelerated stop check on trade and order book prices without waiting for the smoothed average; does not guarantee firing on the first single-tick spike | `UseStopLoss = YES` | Speeds up the exit on a sharp level breakout | In `MoonShot` may lead to an exit on a repeat one-second spike |
| **`TrailingPercent` / `TrailingSpread`** | `Stops` | Watches the price peak after entry and fires on a pullback from the peak by `|TrailingPercent|`; with `UseTakeProfit=YES` raises the chase floor to the `TakeProfit` level (`StopLossSpread` below it) | With `UseTakeProfit=YES` — only while the price is above `TakeProfit` both by the average trade price and by the last trade | Firing starts `Panic Sell` with the `TrailingSpread` spread; the closing price depends on the market | A `TrailingSpread = 0` value is automatically normalized to `0.1%` on load |
| **`UseMarketOrder`** | `Stops` | On any `Panic Sell` of this order (stop, trailing, `BV/SV` stop, exit by filters, formula or key, delisting, manual button) closes with a market order; if the exchange did not fill it — tries to close with a limit order and runs the regular `Panic Sell` | Binance / Bybit / Gate / Bitget, spot and futures; not for a short on spot | Reduces the risk of a limit order getting stuck on a fast order book breakout; closing is not guaranteed | On other exchanges (including OKX) the market stop is not used — always a limit `Panic Sell` |
| **`StopLossSpread` / `AllowedDrop`** | `Stops` | Set the initial offset from the lower of the bid price and recent trade prices, and the extreme boundary for lowering the `Panic Sell` limit price (with `AllowedDrop < -98` the lower floor is disabled) | — | — | Too-wide values go outside the exchange price corridor (`-1013 PERCENT_PRICE_BY_SIDE` / `-4024` / `51031`) |
