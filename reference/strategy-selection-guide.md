## 1. Which algorithm to choose for your trading task

The Moonbot terminal offers **23 working strategy types (`SignalType`)**. Some of them place limit orders in the exchange order book in advance, others look for anomalies in the trade stream and the order book in real time, and others work as signal scouts, protective shields or gateways for external sources (`TradingView`, `Telegram`, the `Hyperliquid` blockchain).

Use this table to go straight from a trading idea to the mechanics, the exact parameters and a ready-made template:

## 2. Full reference of all 23 strategy types (`SignalType`)

Below are all valid values of the `SignalType` parameter in the `##Begin_Strategy ... ##End_Strategy` block (in the form the import accepts them). Note: the value `OrderBook` does not exist for `SignalType` (`OrderBook` is the sort mode `MShotSortBy = OrderBook` in the `MoonShot` strategy), and the order book wall search strategy is called **`WallsDetection`** in the config.

### Group A. Spike hunting and order book scalping

| `SignalType` value | Algorithm purpose | Orders in a calm market | Key setup parameters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`MoonShot`** | Pre-emptive catching of one-second spikes with a limit order placed in the order book in advance and dynamically following the price in a corridor | Sit in the order book and get moved | `MShotPriceMin`, `MShotPrice`, `MShotAdd*Delta`, `MShotAddDistance`, `MShotUsePrice`, `MShotReplaceDelay`, `FastShotAlgo` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#1-moonshot-strategy--pre-emptive-spike-hunting)) |
| **`MoonHook`** | Picking up repeat spikes and bounces after a spike has occurred and the price pullback is confirmed (PRO with MoonBonus) | None (placed only after a detect) | `HookTimeFrame`, `HookDetectDepth`, `HookPriceRollBack`, `HookInterpolate`, `HookInitialPrice`, `HookPriceDistance`, `HookSellLevel` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#2-moonhook-strategy-and-manual-hooks-manual--usehookstrategy)) |
| **`MoonStrike`** | Entry at the moment of the strike — on a breakout of the reference bid line or after the first trade priced above the previous one (a sign that the bottom has stopped updating, not a reversal); requires `AutoBuy = YES` and `OrderSize > 0`; a separate paid strategy | None (placed at the moment of the breakout) | `MStrikeDepth`, `MStrikeVolume`, `MStrikeDirection`, `MStrikeWaitDip`, `MStrikeBuyLevel`, `MStrikeSellLevel` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#3-moonstrike-strategy--instant-entry-on-a-breakout)) |
| **`Spread`** | Trading inside a wide price spread with a dense, continuous trade feed (Autotrading module) | None (placed on a spread detect) | `TimeInterval`, `TradesDensity`, `PriceIntervals`, `PriceSpread`, `BuyPriceInSpread`, `SellPriceInSpread`, `BuyOrderReduce` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#4-spread-and-activity-strategies--trading-inside-the-spread-and-trade-feed-bursts)) |

### Group B. Detectors of impulses, volumes and wave structure

| `SignalType` value | Algorithm purpose | Key setup parameters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`PumpsDetection`** | Search for a sharp directional price rise confirmed by the number and volume of buys over a short window (no shorter than 2 s); on a terminal only the topmost enabled strategy of this type works | `PumpPriceInterval`, `PumpPriceRaise`, `PumpBuysPerSec`, `PumpVolPerSec`, `PumpUsePrevBuyPrice` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#5-dropsdetection-and-pumpsdetection-strategies--sell-offs-and-pumps)) |
| **`DropsDetection`** | Search for a deep price drop over the chosen time window to enter on the reversal correction (the default strategy type when creating) | `DropsMaxTime`, `DropsPriceMA`, `DropsLastPriceMA`, `DropsPriceDelta`, `DropsPriceIsLow` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#5-dropsdetection-and-pumpsdetection-strategies--sell-offs-and-pumps)) |
| **`Volumes`** | Detection of an abnormal burst of buy volume (`BV`) on a short interval relative to a long one, with a check of `BID` order book volume | `VolShortInterval`, `VolLongInterval`, `VolBvShortToLong`, `VolBvToSvShort`, `volBidsDeep`, `volBidsToAsks` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#7-volumes-volumeslite-waves-and-delta-strategies--volume-and-wave-analysis)) |
| **`VolumesLite`** | Assessment of consecutive acceleration of price and volumes on 4 time segments (`T0..T3`) with protection against single spikes | `VLiteT0..T3`, `VLiteP1..P3`, `VLiteV1..V3`, `VLiteDelta0`, `VLiteMaxSpike` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#7-volumes-volumeslite-waves-and-delta-strategies--volume-and-wave-analysis)) |
| **`Waves`** | Search for wave patterns of alternating rise and correction of price and volume on 4 intervals (`T0..T3`) | `WavesT0..T3`, `WavesP1..P3`, `WavesV1..V3`, `WavesDelta0`, `WavesMaxSpike` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#7-volumes-volumeslite-waves-and-delta-strategies--volume-and-wave-analysis)) |
| **`Delta`** | Comparison of the price delta and volume growth on a long interval with the direction of the last short impulse | `DeltaInterval`, `DeltaShortInterval`, `DeltaPrice`, `DeltaVol`, `DeltaVolRaise`, `DeltaLastPrice` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#7-volumes-volumeslite-waves-and-delta-strategies--volume-and-wave-analysis)) |
| **`Activity`** | Detector of a market maker's algorithmic activity by the concentration of identical trade sizes (a machine with ≥2 cores, Autotrading module) | `MMTimeFrame`, `MMOrderMin`, `MMOrderMax`, `MMOrderStep` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#4-spread-and-activity-strategies--trading-inside-the-spread-and-trade-feed-bursts)) |

### Group C. Analysis of the exchange order book, liquidations and market leaders

| `SignalType` value | Algorithm purpose | Key setup parameters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`WallsDetection`** | Search for a large concentration of buy orders in the order book after the price has stayed in a narrow corridor: the volume of several `BID` levels at the set depth is summed; the detect does not track an individual order or its "immobility" | `WallsMaxTime`, `WallsPriceDelta`, `WallBuyVolDeep`, `WallBuyVolume`, `WallBuyVolToDailyVol`, `WallSellVolToBuy`, `WallSellVolDeep` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#6-wallsdetection-and-chartwall-strategies--order-book-walls)) |
| **`ChartWall`** | Experimental wall detect on an open chart: fires when the total volume of one side of the order book within a `WallSpread %` band from the best price exceeds `WallSize`. Hidden from the type list until `[Special] Use_sk_ChartWall` is enabled in `MoonBot.ini`; there is no "at the wall" order tracking — the order is placed in the usual way | `WallSize`, `WallSpread`, `RedWall` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#6-wallsdetection-and-chartwall-strategies--order-book-walls)) |
| **`Liquidations`** | Detector of cascades of forced exchange liquidations with a volume filter, a quiet-period pause and `BV/SV` balance (PRO; futures with a supported liquidation feed, including Binance and Bybit) | `LiqTime`, `LiqCount`, `LiqVolumeMin`, `LiqWaitTime`, `LiqDirection`, `LiqSameDirection`, `Liq_BV_SV_Filter` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#8-liquidations-combo-topmarket-and-alerts-strategies)) |
| **`TopMarket`** | Automatic selection of the leader coin with the largest 15-minute range (no less than `DeltaMin`) and choice of direction by the deviation of the leader's smoothed price from the hourly average | `DeltaMin`, `TMSameDirection` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#8-liquidations-combo-topmarket-and-alerts-strategies)) |
| **`Combo`** | Sequential link of two strategies: fires when `ComboEnd` fires after `ComboStart` within the `ComboDelayMin..Max` window | `ComboStart`, `ComboEnd`, `ComboDelayMin`, `ComboDelayMax` ([details](params/02-detectors-and-signals.md#8-liquidations-combo-topmarket-and-alerts-strategies)) |
