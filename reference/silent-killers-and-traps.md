# Critical strategy configuration errors, parameter limits and atypical Moonbot scenarios

> **Purpose of this document:** A consolidated reference of critical configuration errors, limits on valid value ranges and atypical trading scenarios in the Moonbot terminal (`v7.71`).  
> **Official Moonbot documentation:** [Documentation](https://moonbot.pro/documentation) | [Education](https://moonbot.pro/education)

---

## 1. Risk management and order execution errors

| No. | Parameter / Scenario | Common misconception | Actual terminal behavior (`v7.71`) | Consequence and correct setting |
| :- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Stop loss `-50% .. -90%` in a `Short` strategy** | "A `-80%` stop in a short will close the trade when 80% of the order size is lost" | The price level in `Short` is computed by the formula `P_entry / (1 + d/100)`. A **`-50%`** stop corresponds to the asset price rising **2 times (`+100%` of price, `-100%` of position notional before leverage)**, a **`-80%`** stop — to a **5-fold rise (`+400%` of price, `-400%` of notional)**, a **`-90%`** stop — to a **10-fold rise (`+900%`)**. | Liquidation of the position (the loss at `-80%` exceeds the initial order notional 4 times). In a short keep the main stop enabled with a moderate `StopLoss`, set `StopAboveLiq > 0` and, where the exchange supports it, `UseMarketOrder = YES`. **Do not tighten** `AllowedDrop` in a short: it is the chase limit of the closing order, not a loss level — a tight `AllowedDrop` on a squeeze will leave it below the market ([chapter 06](../concepts/06-orders-exits-and-stops-cascade.md)). |
| **2** | **Wide `StopLossSpread` / `AllowedDrop` on an impulse (`-1013` / `-4024` / `51031`)** | "I'll set `AllowedDrop = -50%` and `StopLossSpread = 10%` for a guaranteed sell into any order book" | `Panic Sell` moves the limit price step by step deeper into the order book. As soon as it leaves the exchange's allowed corridor around the reference price (`PERCENT_PRICE_BY_SIDE` `-1013` on Binance Spot, `-4024` "below the multiplier floor" on Binance Futures, `51031` on OKX), the exchange **rejects the order** — the exit step is lost until the terminal pulls the price back to the corridor boundary or the price itself returns into it. | Enable `UseMarketOrder = YES` (with automatic fallback to a limit order when the exchange refuses), and keep `StopLossSpread` and `AllowedDrop` within the exchange corridor. |
| **3** | **The condition `> -888` inside the `SellByCustomEMA` field** | "I'll put `EMA(5m, 1s) > -888` in `SellByCustomEMA` to record EMA values in the report on exit" | `SellByCustomEMA` closes the position when the formula is true; the collector threshold `> -888` is always true — and the exit condition is met already at the moment of entry. | With `SellEMACheckEnter = YES` (default) the strategy will not buy a single coin for which the formula has data; with `NO` the position closes right after the purchase (the site describes this as a technique for emulator scouts). Collect telemetry only in `CustomEMA`. |
| **4** | **A missing minus sign in `MAX(X, 1s)` (`MAX(60m, 1s) < 5` instead of `< -5`)** | "I want to buy when the price has dropped more than 5% from the hourly maximum" | When the current price is below the maximum, the value of `MAX(X, Y)` is **negative** (`<= 0`). The condition `MAX(60m, 1s) < 5` is true for all trading pairs that have not yet made a new maximum `+5%` higher. | An `EMA` strategy with this condition signals on almost all coins — everyone that is not 5% above its maximum. A pullback from the maximum is always written as a negative threshold; for the level of the last hour write `60m`, not `1h` — hourly windows do not include the last hour. |
| **5** | **`IgnoreFilters = YES` (or `IgnoreBase = YES`) while using the `CustomEMA` entry filter** | "I'll enable `IgnoreFilters = YES` in a `MoonHook` or `DropsDetection` strategy to disable only the deltas but keep the `CustomEMA` filter" | The `CustomEMA` field belongs to the base filter subgroup (`Filters / Base`) and **is skipped in all strategies (except `SignalType = EMA`, where the formula is checked by the detector itself — but then `NextDetectPenalty` and `StrategyPenalty` stop acting) when `IgnoreFilters = YES` or `IgnoreBase = YES` is enabled**. | With `IgnoreFilters = YES` or `IgnoreBase = YES`, the `MoonHook`, `MoonShot`, `DropsDetection` and other strategies stop checking `CustomEMA`. Disable unneeded tabs with targeted flags (`IgnoreDelta = YES`, `IgnoreVolume = YES`), leaving `IgnoreFilters = NO` and `IgnoreBase = NO`. |
| **6** | **A key missing from `TriggerKeysBL` while `SellByTriggerBL` is used** | "I'll specify key `101` only in `SellByTriggerBL` and leave `TriggerKeysBL` empty" | The `SellByTriggerBL` check **runs only for keys that are present in `TriggerKeysBL`**. If the key is not listed in `TriggerKeysBL`, the `SellByTriggerBL` condition **is not checked at all and does not fire**! | When the emergency key `101` arrives, the emergency position dump will not start. Every key from `SellByTriggerBL` **must** also be present in `TriggerKeysBL` with a non-zero blocking time (`TriggerSecondsBL > 0` or `101=300`). |
| **7** | **The Pencil (`Ctrl+LMB` drawing) with live strategies active** | "I'll draw a line on the chart to test the detector; since the tool has the Emulator checkbox ticked, it's safe" | A line with the **Emulator** checkbox is fed into the coin's trade feed as real trades. The tool does not check whether the terminal's emulation mode is on. | All running strategies see the drawn impulse; a strategy without the emulator will respond with a **real order**. Before drawing, enable the terminal's global emulation mode (in a client + kernel setup — on the kernel: the client's local emulation does not protect the kernel) or draw on a separate terminal; after the experiment, reload the charts — the coin's deltas remember the drawn price. |
| **8** | **Auto-selling stuck positions on more than 1 terminal of an account** | "I'll enable auto-closing of stuck positions on all 3 terminals of one sub-account at once, for reliability" | All 3 terminals will simultaneously detect a position without their own order and send closing orders. | The first terminal will close the position to `0`, and the second and third **will open a double position in the opposite direction (`Short`)**. Auto-sell in the position control module must be enabled strictly on **one terminal per account**. |
| **9** | **A shared API key for the trading terminal and the control module (`WatchDog`) on Hyperliquid** | "I'll specify the same Hyperliquid wallet private key in the trading terminal and in the control module" | On Hyperliquid, every request requires a strictly increasing `nonce` counter for each `Agent Wallet`. | A background request from the control module breaks the `nonce` sequence of the trading terminal, after which the exchange rejects closing orders. Always create a separate `API Wallet` for each instance. |
| **10** | **`ActiveTrigger = YES` on a master `MoonShot` that passes `TriggerKeyBuy`** | "I'll enable `ActiveTrigger = YES` so that the key is held while the position is open" | `ActiveTrigger = YES` holds the key only while the master strategy is waiting for a buy (a real `Buy` order or a virtual `BuyStop` / `BuyLimit` is standing), not while the position is open; in `MoonShot` this is the **`TriggerKeyBuy`** key, **which stays active the whole time the limit `Buy` order stands in the order book — until it fills**. | Slave add-on strategies (`EMA-Slave`) start opening market trades before the master `MoonShot` order itself has filled. |
| **11** | **Futures account position mode (`One-Way` / `Hedge Mode`)** | "In `One-Way` the terminal cannot place `reduceOnly`, so `Hedge Mode` is always needed" | In `One-Way`, the terminal itself places closing orders with `reduceOnly` where there is a risk of flipping the position: right after a liquidation and in the first seconds after an exchange `ADL`. On Bybit and Gate the terminal itself switches the account to one-way mode. There is no universal "only `Hedge Mode`" rule. | Choose the position mode for your setup. On the control bot it must match the mode of the working bots of the same account — otherwise the exchange will reject its closing order ([chapter 07](../concepts/07-kernel-vps-and-infrastructure.md)). |
| **12** | **Inheriting stop-loss and take-profit parameters through a shared `JoinSellKey`** | "Each strategy has its own stops, and `JoinSellKey` just merges the position volume" | When orders are merged by `JoinSellKey`, Moonbot assigns the merged order **the first running strategy (started with the Start button) with this key, and if none is running — the first strategy ticked in the terminal's list**; the order inherits its `SellPrice`, `StopLoss` and the name in the report. | If you disable the working strategy or put another strategy with the same `JoinSellKey` higher in the list, the merged order will inherit another strategy's stop and take-profit parameters. |

## 2. Causes of a complete entry block for a strategy

| No. | Parameter / Scenario | Why there are no trades | Solution |
| :- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **13** | **`MaxVolume = 0` or `MaxHourlyVolume = 0` (and also the default `MaxVolume = 100000`)** | For upper volume bounds, the value `0` literally means **"maximum allowed volume 0 dollars"** (blocks 100% of pairs), and the constructor default `MaxVolume = 100000` (`100k`) cuts off all liquid markets. | Set `MaxVolume = 100000M`, `MaxHourlyVolume = 100000M` explicitly. |
| **14** | **`FundingBefore > 0` or `FundingAfter > 0` on a futures market** | Positive values `FundingBefore > 0` / `FundingAfter > 0` mean **"allow entry ONLY within N seconds before/after funding"** (while negative `< 0` values set a pause around the clearing; on a spot market the funding check is skipped automatically). | In regular futures strategies keep `FundingBefore = 0` and `FundingAfter = 0` (and for a protective pause around funding set negative values, for example `-60`). |
| **15** | **An emulator scout slows down live strategies through the coin penalty** | A losing virtual trade of the scout turns on the `TradePenaltyTime` (after one) and `PenaltyTime` (after three in a row) pauses on the coin for all strategies where they are set, and closing the trade activates `TriggerKeyProfit` / `TriggerKeyLoss`. In addition, a closed virtual trade is added to the coin's market session and moves `SessionProfitMin / Max` of the live strategies (the global "Ignore Emulator Trades" checkbox does not affect this); the strategy's own session is a separate counter. Emulator trades are not merged by `JoinSellKey`. | On spot, the scout can have selling disabled (`AutoSell = NO`) — the trade will not close, and there will be no closing events (`TriggerKeyProfit` / `TriggerKeyLoss`, penalties); the buy event (`TriggerKeyBuy`) still fires — this is what the link is built on. On futures `AutoSell` is hidden and forced to `YES`: there, after firing, the scout holds the virtual position until it closes and does not re-arm on this coin; account for `TradePenaltyTime` / `PenaltyTime` in the live strategies. |
| **16** | **A timer `> 864000` after the `=` sign in `TriggerByKey` (`1=999999`)** | The parser of the individual key lifetime discards values above 10 days (`864 000` s), so the trigger does not activate. | Set a lifetime of no more than `864000` (for example, `1=86400`). |
| **17** | **Clearing triggers in `ClearTriggerKeys` with a zero event key (`TriggerKey* = 0`) and with `TriggerAllMarkets = YES`** | 1) The `ClearTriggerKeys` mechanism is invoked only when the strategy itself has a non-zero key for the corresponding event (`TriggerKey > 0` on detect, `TriggerKeyBuy > 0` on buy, etc.).<br>2) With `TriggerAllMarkets = YES`, an attempt to clear its own `TriggerKey` via `ClearTriggerKeys` does not work, because the global activation loop immediately writes this key to all markets again. | In the clearing strategy, specify a free reserve number `TriggerKey = 199` that is not in the `ClearTriggerKeys` list. |
| **18** | **Several strategies of the same type with `IndependentSignals = NO` (default)** | For `DropsDetection`, `Volumes`, `VolumesLite`, `Waves`, `Delta`, `WallsDetection`, `Liquidations`, `MoonStrike` and `Telegram` with `IndependentSignals = NO`, the first strategy from the top that fires interrupts the check of the others of the same type on the current tick. For `EMA`, `Spread`, `MoonHook`, `Activity` and `NewListing` independence is always on. | Enable **`IndependentSignals = YES`** on all strategies of the listed types that must be checked in parallel. |
| **19** | **`DetectModifier > 0` with a narrow `HookDetectDepth .. HookDetectDepthMax` range** | The volatility modifier raises the lower `HookDetectDepth` threshold but does not shift the upper limit `HookDetectDepthMax`. When the computed lower threshold exceeds the upper one, `MoonHook` stops registering spikes. | Raise the upper ceiling `HookDetectDepthMax = 50..100` when using `DetectModifier`. |
| **20** | **Omitted fields on import (`Delta_3h_Max = 20`, `AutoCancelBuy = 300`) or zeroing `Delta_*_Max = 0` / `PriceStepMax = 0`** | 1) Omitted lines take the defaults: `Delta_3h_Max = 20%` cuts off volatile pairs, and `AutoCancelBuy = 300` removes pending orders after 5 minutes.<br>2) An attempt to "disable" a filter by writing `Delta_3h_Max = 0`, `Delta_BTC_Max = 0` or `PriceStepMax = 0` sets an upper limit of `0%` and **completely blocks a rising market**! | To widen the bounds, explicitly specify **`Delta_3h_Max = 1000`**, **`Delta_BTC_Min = -50`**, **`Delta_BTC_Max = 50`**, **`PriceStepMax = 100`** (or `IgnoreDelta = YES` / `IgnorePrice = YES`) and **`AutoCancelBuy = 0`**. |
| **20a** | **Writing zero into the `SellByCustomEMA` field (`SellByCustomEMA = 0`)** | The `SellByCustomEMA` field takes a string logical formula. The character `0` is recognized as a syntax error and blocks entries of the whole strategy (while the `Base` filters are enabled). | To disable `SellByCustomEMA`, leave the field value **empty** (`SellByCustomEMA=`). |
| **20b** | **Seconds in the first argument of `MIN(10s, Y)` / `MAX(10s, Y)` and `k`/`m` suffixes in `Vol`** | 1) The first argument of `MIN`/`MAX` takes only minutes (`m`) or hours (`h`) `>= 5m`: `10s` is automatically turned into **`10m`**.<br>2) To the right of the comparison sign in `Vol(X, Y) > 100000`, the `k`/`m` suffixes are not supported (`100k` will cause `Syntax error!`). | For second-scale windows use `Avg`/`EMA`; write thresholds in `CustomEMA` as full numbers without `k`/`m`. |
| **20c** | **Disabling a strategy with the checkbox while a `MultiOrders` grid is open** | When the next leg of the grid fills and the take-profit orders are joined (`Join`), the terminal looks for the first running strategy with the same `JoinSellKey` (and if there is none — the first ticked one). If you disable the strategy and untick it, the order loses its binding to its `StopLoss` and `Trailing`. | Pause new entries via `AutoBuy = NO` or `sgStop` without removing the strategy's activity until the grid is fully closed. |

## 3. Position tracking specifics and API limit consumption

| No. | Parameter / Scenario | How the terminal works | Solution |
| :- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **21** | **`PriceToSwitch2Stop = 0` when switching to the second stop by timer** | Switching to `SecondStopLoss` requires the `TimeToSwitch2Stop` **and** `PriceToSwitch2Stop` conditions to be met simultaneously. At `0%` the terminal waits for the price to be on the favorable side of the entry (in a long — bid above entry, in a short — below the mirrored level) and does not enable the second stop in a drawdown. | Set a negative price threshold: **`PriceToSwitch2Stop = -10.0`** (or `-50.0`). |
| **22** | **A small `TrailingSpread` step (`0` / `0.1%`) with `Trailing` enabled** | After the trailing line is crossed, the initial `Panic Sell` step is taken from `TrailingSpread` (a value of `0` is automatically normalized to the minimum `0.1%` on load). On a sharp squeeze the minimum `0.1%` step may not be enough to break through the order book immediately. | Keep `TrailingSpread` within the site-recommended `0.4..1.5%` (or enable `UseMarketOrder = YES`). |
| **23** | **`PriceDownAllowedDrop = -100` (or `-1000`) on `Short`** | When the price in a short position is computed by the formula `P_entry / (1 + d/100)`, a value `<= -100` drives the denominator to `<= 0` and blocks `PriceDown`. | Set `PriceDownAllowedDrop = 0` (down to the entry price) or `-5 .. -15`. |
| **24** | **Enabling `SellLevel` (`SellLevelCount > 1`) and `PriceDown` at the same time** | `SellLevel` re-placements are not counted as a manual take-profit move and do not restart the timer from the placement of the `Sell` order, so after `PriceDownTimer` expires both algorithms work in parallel: `PriceDown` lowers the order price by `PriceDownPercent`, and the next `SellLevel` attempt returns it to its own level again until `SellLevelCount` is exhausted. | Use either `PriceDown` or `SellLevel` (or limit `SellLevelCount = 1` until `PriceDownTimer` starts). |
| **25** | **A narrow `MoonShot` corridor on volatile altcoins** | A corridor of a few hundredths of a percent on a volatile coin means hundreds of re-placements per minute and the risk of exchange restrictions (`-1015` / `-4400`). The terminal has no separate "minimum corridor width" — you set the width yourself. | Keep the corridor wider and use `MShotAddDistance` so that it widens on bursts. |
| **26** | **Wrong bounds `MShotPriceMin > MShotPrice`** | On save and load the terminal forcibly sets `MShotPrice = MShotPriceMin + 0.001%`, turning the wrong bounds into an ultra-thin corridor `0.001%` wide with continuous order re-placements. | Always check that `MShotPriceMin < MShotPrice` holds with a working gap. |
| **27** | **Several `PumpsDetection` strategies — only the first works** | The pump detect is computed for one strategy — the topmost enabled `PumpsDetection`. The other strategies of this type never fire, with any settings; this type has no `IndependentSignals` either. | If you need different pump thresholds — change the parameters of the one strategy, use another detect type or a separate terminal. |
| **28** | **An active `ChartWall` strategy blocks neighboring strategies through `GlobalDetectPenalty`** | The `ChartWall` strategy fires on every order book update while a large order is held in it, and continuously updates the time of the coin's last global detect, blocking all strategies with `GlobalDetectPenalty > 0`. | Do not use `GlobalDetectPenalty > 0` on pairs where a `ChartWall` strategy is running. |
| **29** | **`ClearTriggersBelow = YES` does not reset the strategy's own `TriggerKey`** | The `ClearTriggersBelow` check uses the strict inequality `< TriggerKey`: with `TriggerKey = 2` only key `1` is reset, while key `2` itself stays active. | To reset an exact list of keys (for example, `1` and `2`), use the parameter **`ClearTriggerKeys = 1 2`**. |
| **30** | **`Watcher`: coins outside the Coins table and an extra `AutoBuy` "for the kernel"** | 1) `Watcher` is subscribed to the address's trades, not to the coin: you do not need to open a chart, but the coin must be in the terminal's Coins table — otherwise a whale trade on it will pass the strategy by.<br>2) In a kernel + client setup, all ticked strategies detect on the kernel regardless of `AutoBuy`; the client only displays trades. There is no need to enable `AutoBuy = YES` "to make it work on the kernel" — it will enable trading that you may not have planned. | Keep the tracked coins in the Coins table; check that the strategy is ticked on the kernel; set `AutoBuy` only where a purchase is needed. |
| **31** | **`FastShotAlgo = YES` with `SellDelay = 0` closes the trade at a loss on the same spike tick** | With `FastShotAlgo = YES` the closing `Sell` order is placed instantly; if the take-profit is close to the entry price or a fast `PriceDown` is enabled, with `SellDelay = 0` it may fill at a loss within the same impulse. | Set a small **`SellDelay`** (in milliseconds) to let the price finish the impulse and start bouncing before the take-profit is placed. |
| **32** | **`FastShotAlgo = YES` with `MShotReplaceDelay = 0` misses stepped spikes** | Without a re-placement delay, the fast algorithm pulls the order away on the very first micro-pullback and misses the main move of 2–3 consecutive trades. | Keep `FastShotAlgo = YES` and set a small **`MShotReplaceDelay`** — the terminal tooltip names values of about `0.032–0.064` s for mid-range shots. |
| **33** | **Automatic clamping of parameter ranges and rounding of `k/M` suffixes** | On save and load of a strategy the terminal automatically brings values within the allowed bounds: `HookTimeFrame` is clamped to `0.4..10.0` s, `SellSpreadDistance` to `-100..-1` (a positive number is converted to `-1`), `TradesDensity` to `10..100`, `Dyn_Refresh >= 5` s, `AutoCancelBuy >= 15` s in `MoonShot`, the `DontWriteLog`/`DontKeepOrdersOnChart` options are disabled when `EmulatorMode = NO`, and integers `> 10000` are rounded with a `k`/`M` suffix when written to file (`12345 -> 12k -> 12000`). | Set parameter values within the allowed working ranges. |
| **34** | **`NO_CHANGE` on Gate Spot when re-placing `Panic Sell` on penny coins (fixed)** | If the rounded re-placement price matches the old one (Gate changes the price step for coins cheaper than `0.00001`), the exchange responds `{"label":"NO_CHANGE","message":"No change is made"}`. In old builds this sent `Panic Sell` into a re-placement loop; in current versions such a response counts as a successful re-placement. | Update the terminal; on low-priced Gate pairs account for the stepped change of the price step. |
| **35** | **Loss of accumulated `strat.bin` session statistics when a strategy is deleted and pasted again** | Session counters in the `data\*-strat.bin` files are bound to the strategy's internal 64-bit identifier `FIntID`. Deleting a strategy and pasting it from text without preserving `FIntID` creates a new identifier and resets the session binding. | Edit the existing strategy in place, or export/import the configuration preserving `FIntID`. |
| **36** | **A specific filter hidden in "Filters on charts" (`Show Filters`) when `DynWL` is enabled** | When the dynamic whitelist `DynWL` is used, the strategy filters are checked twice: first when `DynWL` is built, then before the order is placed. If a coin failed a filter at the `DynWL` build stage, "Filters on charts" shows only the message "not in dynamic whitelist" without naming the specific filter. | For precise filter diagnostics on the chart, temporarily disable `DynWL` (`DynWL_Count = 0`). |
| **37** | **Refusal to close a position at market at the open interest cap on Hyperliquid (`openInterestCapCanceled`, `tooAggressiveAtOpenInterestCapRejected`)** | While the coin's open interest is at the exchange ceiling (`Open Interest Cap`), HL rejects an order priced more aggressively than the oracle price (`Oracle Price`) — **including a closing one** — and cancels such orders already standing. A market close (`Market Sell`, a deep `Panic Sell`) therefore does not go through. On a sell, the terminal itself pulls the price closer to the last trade and retries. | Close manually with a limit order (the **`Order`** button, not `Market`) priced no more aggressively than `Oracle Price`: for a long — not lower, for a short — not higher. |
| **38** | **Exhausting the cumulative request quota on Hyperliquid** | On Hyperliquid, the address's request quota (a starting reserve of `10 000` plus `1` request per USDC of turnover) **does not recover over time**: every action spends it, and only turnover or buying requests replenish it. The main trap at the end of the quota: a cancellation still goes through but a new order no longer does — when the take-profit is re-placed via cancel and new placement, **the position is left without a closing order** (`400 Too many cumulative requests sent`). | The terminal watches the reserve itself: when it is low, it cancels buys, stops strategies and sends a link where to trade volume or buy more requests ([error explanation](exchange-errors-and-logs.md)). Do not keep `MoonShot` on dozens of coins for hours without trades — this is what eats the quota. |
| **39** | **Volumes not counted in Bybit tournaments (`WSOT`) with `WebSocket API` enabled** | The Bybit exchange does not count the volume of trades sent through the `WebSocket API` trading gateway in tournament statistics. | For the duration of Bybit tournaments, disable the trading `WebSocket API` option in the exchange settings in the terminal (switching to the REST API). |
| **40** | **`DontSellBelowLiq` and `StopAboveLiq` — two different protections with different scope** | `DontSellBelowLiq = YES` acts **only on isolated margin with leverage above 1**: there it keeps `Panic Sell` and `PriceDown` from lowering the closing order below the computed liquidation level (`Stop level is below than liq level; will replace to liq level only`) — on a deep spike the order freezes above the market. On cross margin and on spot the parameter does nothing. `StopAboveLiq > 0` works on futures in any margin mode and has two roles: it pulls the main stop up to the exchange liquidation price (requires the stop to be enabled) and limits the lowering of the take-profit by the `PriceDown` algorithm. | Decide in advance what matters more on a spike — closing at any price or not selling into an empty order book: with `DontSellBelowLiq = YES` on isolated margin the position may stay open. Do not expect `DontSellBelowLiq` to protect you on cross margin. |
| **41** | **Using `50x` leverage on volatile Binance Futures altcoins with `MMR = 1.5%`** | At `50x` leverage the initial margin is `100% / 50 = 2.0%`, while the maintenance margin rate (`Maintenance Margin Rate`, `MMR`) on volatile Binance altcoins is **`1.5%`**. The buffer before forced isolated liquidation is only a **`0.5%` price move**. | Do not use `50x` leverage on isolated margin for altcoins with a high `MMR`; use `Cross` margin on a dedicated sub-account and a working leverage of `10x–20x`. |
| **42** | **Choosing `CUSTOM` instead of `XYZ` on Hyperliquid `HIP-3` futures (`BRENTOIL`)** | If you select `CUSTOM` instead of `XYZ` in the base asset selector, the terminal interprets the market as spot and limits the maximum futures order size by the **account's spot balance**. | To trade contracts of the `XYZ` section, select the **`XYZ`** mode on the base currency button. |
| **43** | **The `Vol(X, Y) > 0` threshold for writing volume to the report in `CustomEMA`** | In current Moonbot versions (`v7.71`) the only collector threshold for the `Vol` function is **`Vol(X, Y) > 0`**, while any other numeric thresholds (including values `> 887`, for example `Vol(0s, 30s) < 62000`) are checked as regular working filters. | Use `Vol(X, Y) > 0` to log volume (at least one trade in the window is required, otherwise the condition is false) into the trade report, and specify real numeric bounds for market filtering. |
| **44** | **Opening a spot short (`Spot Short`) with `Pump` (`AutoSell = NO`) and `MoonHook` (`HookDirection = Both`) running together** | If on a spot market a strategy with `AutoSell = NO` bought coins to the balance, and then `MoonHook` with `HookDirection = Both` fired, it can open a **spot short by selling the previously bought coins from the balance**. | On spot terminals, when using strategies with `AutoSell = NO`, always set the direction **`HookDirection = OnlyLong`** in `MoonHook`. |
| **45** | **`PriceDownDelay = 0` with `PriceDownTimer > 0` enabled** | The first lowering of the take-profit price happens `PriceDownTimer` seconds after the `Sell` order is placed, and all subsequent steps — every `PriceDownDelay` seconds. With `PriceDownDelay = 0` there is no pause between steps: after the first step the `Sell` order is lowered as often as the terminal manages, down to `PriceDownAllowedDrop`. | To lower the `Sell` order by `PriceDown` exactly once, set a deliberately large value **`PriceDownDelay = 9999`**. |
| **46** | **An excessive total `Panic Sell` offset and expecting a fallback floor with `UseStopLoss = NO`** | 1) The `Panic Sell` offset is the sum of `StopLossSpread` (clamped to `0.1..10%`) and the `StopSpreadAdd1mDelta` adjustment from the coin's one-minute range. On a coin with a huge range and a small order (`15$`), the computed price falls so low that the order sum (`Price * Quantity`) becomes less than the exchange minimum (`MIN_NOTIONAL`) — the refusal `Panic Sell: Too low quantity, cant replace` (a total offset of `111%` has been seen in the log).<br>2) A disabled stop (`UseStopLoss = NO`) creates no fallback floor: the depth of an emergency sell (`SellByFilters`, `SellByTriggerBL`, manual `Panic Sell`) is determined only by the strategy's `AllowedDrop` (default `-99` — no floor). | Keep `StopLossSpread` within the site-recommended `0.4..1.5%` and do not inflate `StopSpreadAdd1mDelta` on volatile coins; explicitly set the allowed depth `AllowedDrop` for emergency exits. |
