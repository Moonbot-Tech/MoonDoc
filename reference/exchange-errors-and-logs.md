# Decoding `logs/LOG_YYYY-MM-DD.log` entries and exchange error codes

> **Purpose of this document:** A reference of exchange response codes and diagnostic messages in the Moonbot terminal log (`v7.71`).  
> **Official Moonbot documentation:** [Documentation](https://moonbot.pro/documentation) | [Education](https://moonbot.pro/education)  
> **Related chapters:** [06. Orders, take-profits and the stop cascade](../concepts/06-orders-exits-and-stops-cascade.md) | [07. Kernel, VPS and infrastructure](../concepts/07-kernel-vps-and-infrastructure.md)

## 1. Exchange error codes (Binance, Bybit, OKX, Gate, Bitget, HTX, Hyperliquid)

| Error code / Exchange response line | Exchange / Market | Meaning on the exchange side | Cause in Moonbot and solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`-1013 Filter failure: PERCENT_PRICE_BY_SIDE`** (or `PERCENT_PRICE`) | Binance Spot | The limit order price is outside the exchange's allowed deviation corridor from the reference price (the average price of recent trades over several minutes) | Occurs: 1) with a too-wide `StopLossSpread` / `AllowedDrop` during a `Panic Sell` on a squeeze; 2) when non-zero `Add*Delta` in `MoonShot` are multiplied by a giant listing delta (`50 000%`).<br>**Solution:** enable `UseMarketOrder = YES` in the stops; do not inflate `StopLossSpread` and `MaxModifier`. |
| **`-4016 Price is higher than mark price multiplier cap`** / **`-4024 Price is lower than mark price multiplier floor`** | Binance Futures (`fapi`) | The limit price left the corridor the exchange builds around `Mark Price` with up and down multipliers: `-4016` — above the cap (a far take-profit in a long, a buy or a short's stop high above the market), `-4024` — below the floor (a deep `MoonShot`, a long's `Panic Sell` on a sell-off) | For closing orders the terminal tries to correct the price from the exchange response and retry the placement; for opening orders — only if order price correction from the exchange response is enabled in the settings, otherwise the order is not placed.<br>**Solution:** `UseMarketOrder = YES` for stops (with auto-fallback to limit) and an adequate `AllowedDrop`; in `MoonShot` do not inflate the depth with modifiers. |
| **`-4400 Quantitative Rules violated`** and the log **`According to new Futures Trading Quantitative Rules you can place only 29 orders per 33 markets. You placed 32 order. Market JASMY will be blackListed for 10 minutes.`** | Binance Futures (`fapi`) | The exchange's quantitative rules are violated (`Unfilled Order Ratio` / `Order-to-Trade Ratio` / `Cancel Ratio`). Since the `GET /fapi/v1/apiTradingStatus` and `GET /fapi/v1/apiTradingStat` endpoints return an empty `{"indicators":{}}` right up to the moment of the ban, Moonbot uses its own pre-protection heuristic (`Settings -> Advanced -> Quantitative Rules`, the `SetQRules ON\OFF` command, the `O10m` / `O:NNN` counters): the order ceiling per coin `10 000 / 1.2^(N − 1)`, where `N` is the number of coins that **currently** have orders or had orders placed in the last minute or two, is compared with **this terminal's** order counter for the coin over the last ~11 minutes | When the ceiling is exceeded, the coin goes into quarantine for 11 minutes ("10 minutes" in the log): new orders on it are not placed, and an already standing `Buy` may be cancelled by the repeated filter check (in `MoonShot` always, in the others with `CheckAfterBuy`). The more coins have orders at the same time, the lower the ceiling for each. This is a Moonbot heuristic, not an exchange rule: it knows only its own terminal's orders (on an account with several bots the orders are not summed) and does not guarantee that the exchange will not restrict the account; the exchange response does not show whether the coin or the whole account is restricted. Before a listing, turn off extra coins in advance — 11 minutes ahead. |
| **`-1015 Too many new orders`** (including a personal cut to **`25 orders per 10 SECOND`** or **`30 orders per MINUTE`**) | Binance Spot / Futures | The account's 10-second (`50–100/10s` Spot, `300/10s` Futures), minute (`1200/1m`) or daily (`160 000–200 000/24h` Spot) order limit is exceeded | The exchange may cut the account's order limit individually — `25 orders per 10 SECOND` and `30 orders per MINUTE` have been seen in the logs; the exchange names the current limit in the response itself (`current limit is …`). The overload protection holds back new orders while still approaching the limit (section 2, `Order won't proceed due to API or CPU overload`).<br>**Solution:** place and cancel orders less often — fewer coins and re-placements; on the daily limit on spot — trade from a neighboring sub-account: the limit is counted per account. |
| **`HTTP 429` / `HTTP 418 I'm a teapot (IP banned)`** | Binance Spot / Futures (REST API); `429` also occurs on other exchanges | `429` — the request limit is exceeded: the request weight from the IP (`Request Weight`) or the number of orders; `418` — the IP is blocked for continuing requests after `429`; the exchange names the block duration in the response | Causes: several bots on one VPS (one IP) or spamming an expired/delisted contract. On Binance the terminal performs an autostop itself: releases Start, stops strategies, cancels buy orders and sends "Auto Stop on API overload has been triggered!". Restart — by the "Restart in N minutes" checkbox on the `Autostart` tab; without it — manually.<br>**Solution:** 1 trading bot = 1 IP; keep the control bot on a separate VPS with a different IP; do not turn off the overload protection (section 2) — it holds back orders before the `429` response arrives. |
| **`-1021 Timestamp for this request is outside of the recvWindow`** | Binance Spot / Futures | The request reached the exchange later than the `recvWindow` window allows (in Moonbot it is set by the `HFT` parameter), or the request timestamp diverged from the exchange clock | On opening `Buy` orders with **`HFT`** (for example, `HFT = 150`) this is **normal protection**: the exchange itself rejected an order that was late due to lag; it does not count as an API error; the terminal reports the rejections in Telegram (`HFT order time fail`, section 2). If the error pours in without `HFT` — after it the terminal re-syncs time with the exchange itself; on repeats check the ping and the server clock synchronization ([chapter 07](../concepts/07-kernel-vps-and-infrastructure.md)). |
| **`-2010 Account has insufficient balance`** / **`-2019 Margin is insufficient`** | Spot / Futures | Not enough free funds or margin to place the order | Three non-obvious causes:<br>1) On **Spot**, `Long` and `Short` strategies work on the same pair at the same time and share one coin balance;<br>2) On **Futures**, a micro-position on the coin is open and you try to place an order with leverage **`> 20x`** (a Binance rule forbids `>20x` with an open position);<br>3) A market click into a deep order book at high leverage reserves margin at the worst price. |
| **`Status: 400 Exceeded the maximum allowable position at current leverage`** | Binance Futures | The table limit of the maximum position at the current leverage is exceeded | On volatile coins, when `MarkPrice` diverges from `LastPrice`, the exchange computes the limit at the unfavorable price and rejects even a `4890$` order with a `5000$` limit.<br>**Solution:** reduce the leverage or the position size; with the `Auto Leverage` checkbox (`Settings → Advanced`) the terminal itself tries to lower the leverage and retry the order (`Auto change Leverage`, section 2). The "Don't check position limits" checkbox does not cure this error: it only allows placing a long and a short at the same time, each up to the limit. |
| **`Status: 400 Symbol is on pre-delivering`** / **`Market is closed`** | Quarterly futures / Delistings | The pair is in the process of expiration or delisting; opening new positions is forbidden by the exchange | If a strategy keeps hammering such a pair, within 2 minutes the IP goes into a `418` ban.<br>**Solution:** the `BinanceTokenTags = !Monitoring` filter and exclusion of expiring contracts. |
| **`51031` (Order price is not within the price limit)** | OKX Spot / Futures | The order's limit price left OKX's hard corridor of allowed prices | On OKX the market stop `UseMarketOrder` is not supported — the stop always goes as a limit `Panic Sell`. On `51031` the terminal itself re-places the closing order up to four times, pulling the price toward the last trade with each attempt.<br>**Solution:** do not inflate `StopLossSpread` and `AllowedDrop` — the deeper the limit `Panic Sell`, the more likely it runs into the OKX corridor. |
| **`12030 The connection with the server was terminated abnormally`** / **`10054`** | Windows network stack / TLS | The exchange server or an intermediate node dropped the TCP/TLS connection while the request was executing | If `12030` arrives at the moment a `Sell` order is sent or re-placed, the terminal does not know whether the request reached the exchange and moves the order to the **`Replacing`** status until it finds out its fate from the exchange data. With the "Ignore the order replacing errors" checkbox (`Settings → Advanced`), the interrupted attempt is counted as failed immediately, and the lost order is left to the control bot — this is how a working bot + control bot pair is set up. |
| **`Invalid API-key, IP, or permissions for action`** | Binance / Bybit / Huobi | The key's trading rights are disabled or the server IP has changed | On Binance, keys **without an IP address specified in the settings** have their trading rights **automatically disabled after 90 days** (the terminal warns 7 days ahead). On Huobi, keys without an IP expire after 60 days. |
| **`RISK_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`** | Gate Futures | The exchange blocked order opening because of a desync of the pair's risk-limit tier on the Gate side | **Solution:** cured by re-applying (moving the slider back and forth) the very same leverage on the coin! |
| **`{"label":"NO_CHANGE","message":"No change is made"}`** | Gate Spot | When an order is re-placed (`Amend`), the new rounded price matched the old one — the exchange responds "no change" | In current versions the terminal counts such a response as a successful re-placement. Historical case: in old builds, during a `Panic Sell` on leveraged **`*3S` / `*5S`** tokens and penny memecoins, when the price on a dump fell **below `0.00001`** and Gate changed the `TickSize` on the fly, this response sent `Panic Sell` into an endless re-placement loop. |
| **`12002` (`dynamic-symbol-list` timeout)** and warnings for **"spoofing"** (`NEWTUSDT`, `STOPUSDT`) | Bybit Spot / Futures | 1) The `12002` timeout error on the `dynamic-symbol-list` request — a timeout loading the trading pair list from the Bybit server;<br>2) Warnings and bans for "spoofing" — Bybit's anti-fraud blocked accounts for frequent `Cancel + New` of limit `Buy` orders on `MoonShot` | For `MoonShot` on Bybit, in July 2025 the re-placement of `Buy` orders was switched from `Cancel + New` to the atomic **`Amend`** modification. |
| **`"status":"openInterestCapCanceled"`**, **`"tooAggressiveAtOpenInterestCapRejected"`** and **`"Error placing new order during modify: Cannot place order 0.01 more aggressive than oracle when open interest is at cap."`** | Hyperliquid (`HL`) | While the coin's open interest is at the exchange ceiling (`Open Interest Cap`), HL does not accept orders that increase the position and rejects an order priced more aggressively than the oracle price (`Oracle Price`) — **including a closing one** (`tooAggressiveAtOpenInterestCapRejected`); too-aggressive orders already standing are cancelled by the exchange with the status `openInterestCapCanceled` (seen on `MAVIA`, `CBRS`). The exchange documentation does not name a numeric tolerance | **The OI cap trap on HL:** a market close (`Market Sell`, a deep `Panic Sell`) will not go through — its price is more aggressive than the oracle. Having received such a rejection on a sell, the terminal itself moves the price closer to the last trade and retries. Close manually with a limit order via the **`Order`** button on the chart (not `Market`), priced no more aggressively than `Oracle Price`: for a long — not lower, for a short — not higher. |
| **`400 Too many cumulative requests sent (10999 > 10148)`** and **`Place taker orders to free up 1 request per USDC traded`** (`PositionControlerV2`) | Hyperliquid (`HL`) | The account's cumulative request limit on Hyperliquid is exhausted: a starting buffer of `10 000` requests + **`1` request per `1$` of traded volume** | The address quota on HL **does not reset by timer**: every action spends it, and only turnover replenishes it (1 request per USDC). The main trap at the end of the quota: cancellations have their own, wider limit, while placement spends the common one — if a take-profit re-placement goes through cancel and new placement, the cancel still goes through but the new order no longer does, and **the position is left without a closing order**. Moonbot watches the remainder itself: when the forecast drops below the reserve (about 10 requests per active order plus a margin), it cancels buy orders, stops strategies and sends a link where to trade volume or buy more requests.<br>**Solution:** trade volume or buy more requests (the terminal sends the link itself); do not keep `MoonShot` on dozens of coins for hours without trades — re-placements without turnover are exactly what eats the quota. |
| **`PRICE_TOO_DEVIATED`** | Gate Futures | The limit price deviated from the mark price (`Mark Price`) more than the Gate Futures allowed corridor | A deep limit `Panic Sell` on a sell-off runs into this corridor; the terminal itself pulls the price inside the corridor and retries, but closing with a market order is more reliable.<br>**Solution:** enable **`UseMarketOrder = YES`** in the strategy (market stop support on Gate Futures was added in `Moonbot-F5`, April 2, 2026). |
| **`-1121 Invalid symbol`** (`marginType`) and **`Status: 410 Gone`** (`POST /api/v3/userDataStream`) | Binance Spot / Futures | 1) `-1121 Invalid symbol` in the `marginType` method — the terminal tries to switch the margin type (`AutoIsolated`) on a coin already delisted from the exchange but left in the strategy's `CoinsWhiteList`;<br>2) `410 Gone` — on February 20, 2026 Binance shut down the old REST endpoint `POST /api/v3/userDataStream` (and on April 23, 2026 — the old WS addresses `wss://fstream.binance.com/ws` and `/stream`) | 1) On `-1121 Invalid symbol`, clear `CoinsWhiteList` of delisted coins;<br>2) On `410 Gone` or no connection to Binance Futures after April 23, 2026, update Moonbot to the current version (`Moonbot-X20+` / `v7.62+`). |
| **`[110123]`** (Bybit `XAU`), **`110090`** (Bybit Leverage), **`170131`** / **`170241`** (Bybit Spot), **`41118`**, **`22029`** and **`40883`** (Bitget) | Bybit / Bitget | 1) On Bybit `110123` / `110125` (`You must agree to the Trading Terms`) and on Bybit Spot **`170241`** (`...confirm that they fully understand the project's risk disclosure`) — the agreement for the instrument (for example, for gold `XAU` or a new coin) has not been accepted on the exchange's website or app; until it is confirmed manually, orders on the coin are not placed — the terminal shows this in the Problems window and, with notifications enabled, in Telegram; **`110090`** (`Order placement failed as your position may exceed the max limit`) — the position limit at the current leverage is exceeded; on Bybit Spot **`170131`** (`Balance insufficient`) — not enough balance; `170212` (`Cancel order request processing`) and `170213` (`Order does not exist`) — the response to cancelling an order already being cancelled or already cancelled; the terminal counts the cancellation as done;<br>2) On Bitget, code `41118` — auto-buy of `BGB` for fees; code `22029` — the exchange's `Risk Control` on a sharp spike; code **`40883`** (`When the currencies are mixed, it cannot be adjusted to the warehouse-by-warehouse mode`) — a refusal to switch from `Cross` to `Isolated` margin while `Multi-Assets` mode is enabled on Bitget | On Bybit, confirm the agreement or risk disclosure for the instrument on the exchange website, use `Auto leverage by max order` (`def 0`) against `110090`; on Bitget, disable `Multi-Assets` mode to switch to `Isolated` (`40883`). |
| **`-4105` / `-4106` / `-4107`**, **`-4401`**, **`-4108`** and **`-1007 Timeout waiting for response from backend server`** | Binance Futures | 1) On [Binance USD-M](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/derivatives-trading-usds-futures/error-code), `-4105` restricts the symbol to position-reducing orders; `-4106` and `-4107` apply that restriction to buys and sells respectively. `-4401` is a large-position restriction that also permits only reduction. After these rejections, Moonbot retries a closing order while attempts remain: with `reduceOnly` in One-Way mode, or its position side in Hedge mode. A retry does not confirm a fill;<br>2) `-4108` — absent from the official code table; seen in the logs with the texts `Server is currently overloaded` (a refusal without placing the order) and `Symbol is on delivering or delivered or pre-trading` (the contract is in delivery);<br>3) `-1007` — the exchange did not respond in time, and **the result is unknown**: the order may or may not have been re-placed; the state is clarified from exchange data (the order stream, lost-order control), but the re-placement must not be assumed to have happened in advance | On `-4105`, `-4106`, `-4107` and `-4401`, do not open a new position. Read the order and the remaining position after the repeated sell; the Problems window names the coin. On `-1007`, wait for the terminal to reconcile the order state and do not duplicate the action manually. |
| **`Auth error! Cant get HL server time`** / **`AuthCheck error! TryCount=3 [200] <undefined> {"type":"info","payload":{"type":"maxBuilderFee","data":0}}`** | Hyperliquid (`HL`) | Two different lines:<br>1) `Cant get HL server time` — in 5 seconds the terminal did not receive the exchange time over WebSocket: this is a problem with the connection to the exchange (network, VPN, access from the region), not with the key;<br>2) `AuthCheck error! … "maxBuilderFee","data":0` — the owner wallet has not signed the builder fee approval (`ApproveBuilderFee`); `AuthCheck error` with a different response also occurs with a replaced or expired `API Wallet` key | On `Cant get HL server time` — check the server's connection to the exchange. At the first HL launch, the builder fee approval must be signed once with the owner wallet (it cannot be done with the agent key) — the terminal opens the signing page itself — and there must be a balance on Perps. `API Wallet` rules on HL: an account has one unnamed and up to three named keys (plus two named ones per sub-account); a new unnamed key replaces the previous unnamed one, a key with the same name replaces its namesake, so creating a key "for another bot" may disable the key of a working one; a key's validity is limited (no more than 180 days). Each bot process gets its own key with its own name. |
| **`[400] {"error":"Only post-only orders allowed during extreme L1 congestion"}`** and **`Invalid nonce: nonce too high`** (`18446744073637503812`) | Hyperliquid (`HL`) | 1) In extreme L1 blockchain congestion mode, HL temporarily accepts only maker `Post-Only` orders (`2025_Q4`);<br>2) `nonce too high` — the key's transaction counter (`nonce`) ran ahead or is corrupted; in the `2025_Q4` case an overflowed 64-bit number went into the authorization request (`AuthCheck error! TryCount=3 [400] <Invalid nonce: nonce too high 18446744073637503812 >`) | On `nonce too high` the terminal corrects the counter itself (`Nonce adjusted: <was> => <now>` in the log); there is no need to recreate the key — a new unnamed `API Wallet` will disable the previous one. If the error repeats — check that the key is not used by two processes (the bot and the control bot): each must have its own. |
| **`{"code":-4120,"msg":"Order type not supported for this endpoint. Please use the Algo Order API endpoints instead."}`** | Binance Futures (`fapi`) | Since December 2025, Binance moved conditional stop/trigger orders (`STOP_MARKET` / `MarketStop`) out of the standard REST order endpoint into a separate **`Algo Order API`** service (while in WebSocket they keep arriving in the common stream with extra fields) | Update Moonbot to the current build (`Moonbot-A3+` from December 2025), where `Algo Service` support is split for REST and WS. |
| **`-4087 INVALID_PAIR`** / **`-4089 REDUCE_ONLY_ORDER_PERMISSION`** | Binance COIN-M (`dapi`) | These codes differ from USD-M: on [COIN-M](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/derivatives-trading-coin-futures/error-code), `-4087` means an invalid pair; `-4089` restricts the account to orders that reduce positions | On `-4087`, check the requested pair. On `-4089`, inspect the account's trading restrictions; new positions are not allowed. |
| **`Status: 400 User can only place reduce only order`** and **`-4168` (`Multi-Assets mode`)** | Binance Futures (`fapi`) | 1) The exchange allows only closing orders (`-4087 REDUCE_ONLY_ORDER_PERMISSION`, `2025_Q4`); the Problems window shows this as a restriction of the whole account; only exchange support knows the reason;<br>2) `-4168` — a `Multi-Assets Mode` collateral mode conflict when switching margin (`2025_Q1`) | On `reduce only`, close positions (in `One-Way` the terminal itself retries the closing order with the `reduceOnly` flag) and find out the reason from Binance support; on `-4168`, check the `Single-Asset / Multi-Assets Mode`. |
| **`[10001] sell closing order price[...] should be higher than buy_pz bust price`** / **`Error buy closing order price[...] should be lower than sell_pz bust price[...] [10001]`** | Bybit Futures | The exchange rejected the closing limit order (or `Panic Sell` / `StopLoss`) because its computed price crossed the position's forced bankruptcy price (`bust price` / the liquidation price `buy_pz` or `sell_pz`) | Occurs when trying to place a stop or a `Panic Sell` step deeper than the liquidation price at high leverage.<br>**Solution:** enable **`DontSellBelowLiq = YES`** and/or set an offset **`StopAboveLiq > 0`** (in `%`), reduce `StopLossSpread` and `AllowedDrop`, or use the market stop `UseMarketOrder = YES`. |
| **`[110021]` (Open Interest limit)** / **`[30228]` (Delisting)** / **`[110013]` (Risk limit leverage)** / **`170209` (Spot market error)** | Bybit Spot / Futures | `[110021]` — the Open Interest ceiling on Bybit is reached;<br>`[30228]` — the coin is in the process of delisting;<br>`[110013]` — the leverage cannot be set because of the risk-limit tier;<br>`170209` — a Bybit spot market restriction (`2025_Q3..Q4`) | Use the `BinanceTokenTags = !Monitoring` filter, reduce the position size/leverage. *(An important specific of Bybit `WSOT` tournaments: volumes of orders sent through the trading **`WebSocket API`** are not counted by Bybit in the tournament turnover — for tournaments this checkbox in the settings must be cleared!)* |
| **`HTTP 403 (Access Denied)`** vs **`"ret_msg": "Too many visits!"`** | Bybit REST API | Two independent Bybit rate limits:<br>1) `HTTP 403` (`access too frequent`) — the request limit from the IP is exceeded: per Bybit documentation, **600 requests per 5 seconds**;<br>2) `"Too many visits!"` (`retCode 10006`) — the account (UID) API limit per sliding second is exceeded; each method has its own cap; the exchange reports the remainder in the response headers | On `HTTP 403` the exchange blocks the IP (often when several bots run on one VPS IP); per the documentation you need to close all HTTP sessions and wait at least 10 minutes — the ban lifts by itself. On `"Too many visits!"` only the overloaded method fails; there is no IP ban. |
| **`ORDER_NOT_FOUND` (`GET /futures/{settle}/orders/{order_id}`)** | Gate Futures | An attempt to request the status of a cancelled order with zero fill later than 10 minutes after cancellation | A Gate engine detail: orders with zero fill (`Zero-fill order`) are kept in the exchange's RAM only **10 minutes after cancellation**, after which they are deleted and return `ORDER_NOT_FOUND`. |
| **`45119` / `40309`** (Bitget) and **`403 ERROR The request could not be satisfied (CloudFront)`** / **`CheckServerStatus error. Check your internet connection. TryCount: 2`** (HTX) | Bitget / HTX (Huobi) | 1) On Bitget (`45119`, `40309`) — contract/leverage restrictions (`2025_Q3`);<br>2) On HTX (`403 CloudFront`) — with `LogEveryRequest` it was established that CloudFront was blocking not the HTX trading API but the **obsolete server status check endpoint `https://status.huobigroup.com/api/v2/summary.json`**, which spammed the log (and the `MoonCMD` UDP stream) with the error **`CheckServerStatus error. Check your internet connection. TryCount: 2`** and an HTML wall of text `403 ERROR ... Generated by cloudfront` | In build **`Moonbot-A2` (December 7, 2025)** the obsolete `CheckServerStatus` request (`status.huobigroup.com`) is fully disabled; for trading on HTX use the current terminal version and a VPS in permitted Asian locations (Tokyo/Singapore). |
| **`Error Code 12007 (ERROR_INTERNET_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED)`** and **`Socket Error # 11001 Host not found`** | Windows network stack (`WinInet` / `WinSock` DNS) | The Windows system resolver could not resolve the exchange name to an address. In one case (`2026_Q2`) the cause was a mixed DNS pair `1.1.1.1` / `8.8.8.8` on the adapter — this is a historical case, not a general diagnosis | **Solution:** first check whether the exchange name resolves from this server (`nslookup`), then the adapter DNS and the exchange's availability from the region; changing DNS is one possible cure, not a universal one. |
| **`Status: 400 Mandatory parameter 'timestamp' was not sent`** / **`Order does not exist (-2013)`** / **`PUT listenKey`** | Binance REST / WS | Transport errors on connection drops or REST/WS desync on lags (`2019_Q4`, `2020_Q1`) | Switch the connection method (`Original` / `Method 2` instead of `Indy`), check time synchronization. |
| **`Price is higher than mark price multiplier cap`** / **`[4024] Limit price can't be lower than...`** (`-4024`, `10.71622`) / **`"Limit price can't be higher than"`** / **`[-2027]`** / **`-2011 Unknown order sent`** / **`Status: 400 Margin is insufficient.`** / **`[1135] Invalid account status`** | Binance Futures (`fapi`) | 1) The computed price of a limit order or a `Panic Sell` step (as well as of the native `Conditional Order` stop) left the exchange's percentage corridor around `MarkPrice` (`-4024`, where on `AXS` with a 3-decimal step the exchange sent 5 decimals `10.71622`, and since `v6.85` rounding in the terminal goes strictly inward into the corridor `10.717`);<br>2) `[-2027]` (`Exceeded the maximum allowable position at current leverage`) — in One-Way mode opposing limit orders and a `Sell` set far above eat the leverage limit; `-2011 Unknown order sent` — the order being cancelled has already filled or fell off after `12030`; `Status: 400 Margin is insufficient.` — since `v6.85` the penalty is tied to the order size `X`; `[1135] Invalid account status` — caused a false `Account ban detected` alert | Reduce `StopLossSpread` and `AllowedDrop`, enable `UseMarketOrder = YES`; on `-2027` — reduce the leverage or the position size. |
| **`Status: 400 Leverage 100 is not valid`** and **`Quantity greater than max quantity`** | Binance Futures (`fapi`) | 1) Leverage above the table maximum for this coin was requested (`2020_Q2`);<br>2) The order volume exceeded the maximum exchange lot (`max quantity`, triggers auto-split; also a limit of no more than **`10` exchange conditional stop orders** applies per coin, `2023_Q1`) | Use `Set Max Leverage` / automatic selection of the maximum leverage by order volume, and `SplitPiece`. |
| **`Status: 503 Internal error; unable to process your request`** | Binance Edge / CDN | An internal error on the exchange side. Per Binance documentation, the text `Internal error; unable to process your request` means the operation was not performed; an unknown result is the `Unknown error, please check your request or try again later` variant. In any case, verify the order state | In `2023_Q1` changing DNS helped to land on another CDN node — a historical case, not a recipe. **Solution:** check the order state from exchange data and retry the operation. |

## 2. Decoding diagnostic entries in `logs/LOG_YYYY-MM-DD.log`

| Line in `logs/LOG_YYYY-MM-DD.log` | Meaning of the diagnostic entry in Moonbot |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`Order won't proceed due to API or CPU overload. CPU: Bot X Sys: Y API Req: ...`** | The built-in overload protection fired (`v5.96+`): the terminal approached its resource limit or the exchange limits. There are several thresholds: the terminal's CPU — above 90% on average or above 96% instantaneously (`Bot`), the whole system's — above 99% (`Sys`, for example because of `TiWorker.exe` / RDP); API request weight — above 88% of the limit (`API Req`); orders — above 90% of the minute or 10-second limit or 95% of the daily one (`API Orders`, `Orders 10S`, `Orders 1D`); main thread latency — more than 5 s (`AppLatency`). While the condition holds, new strategy orders (except manual and `UDP`) are not placed; in `MoonShot` and `MoonHook` a standing `Buy` is cancelled when the terminal tries to re-place it. Strategies are not stopped — this is not the `429` / `418` autostop. |
| **`API latency is too high` / `No trades on markets` / `PumpQ<0!`** | 1) `API latency is too high` — in `VDS` mode fires on a **5-second** pause between price points (`2020_Q2`);<br>2) `No trades on markets` — diagnostics of a trade stream stop (`2025_Q1`, a line from old versions);<br>3) `PumpQ<0!` — a diagnostic marker of the pump quality index in the log (`2021_Q3`). |
| **`UseMarketOrder FAIL, fallback to limit. RestQ: …`** / **`UseMarketOrder canceled, fallback to limit. RestQ: …`** | A stop with `UseMarketOrder = YES` tried to close the position with a market order, but the exchange did not accept it (`FAIL`) or cancelled the unfilled remainder (`canceled`; `RestQ` — how much is left). The terminal immediately places a limit order for the remainder at the last trade price (if not accepted — one tick worse); afterwards the exit is led by the regular limit `Panic Sell`. |
| **`StopLoss AutoActivated on price drop`** | Automatic activation of the stop loss on a sharp price drop relative to the protection threshold. |
| **`Changing Leverage on <COIN>: 40 => 25 (Position Limit)`** | Automatic leverage reduction by the terminal when it hits the table limit of the maximum position at the current leverage. Automatic leverage reduction is blocked while there are active take-profits on the coin, so as not to shift the liquidation price and the `DontSellBelowLiq` boundary during a `Panic Sell`. |
| **`[HookData]` / `Buffer: [PriceHigh..PriceLo]` / `pUP: ... pDown: ...`** | A detailed log of `MoonHook` detect and tracking. In live trading it is enabled by a "secret combo": the `DebugLog = YES` checkbox in the hook + a loosened `MoonStrike` in the emulator (which activates `[HookData]` recording for **15 seconds** after each strike). `Buffer: [PriceHigh..PriceLo]` and `pUP`/`pDown` show the price corridor in which the order sits without re-placement. |
| **`[WatchDog Self] === WSOrder:`** / **`GetOrderAbs`** | Control bot records: it puts the `[WatchDog <name>]` prefix on lines about the account of the bot with that name (`Self` in the example is the bot name), `=== WSOrder:` — an order report from the account's order stream; `GetOrderAbs` — a REST request of the order state when the WebSocket report is delayed. |
| **`AutoIsolated: checking`** / **`ChangePositionType:`** | Background check and automatic switching of the coin's margin mode to isolated (`AutoIsolated` -> `ChangePositionType:`). |
| **`MoonKernel: SendCandlesRequest`** / **`CandlesChunk`** | Exchange of historical candles between the `MoonKernel` kernel and the UI client when a chart is opened. |
| **`FreeDisk:` / `PageFile:` / `UseMem:` / `PrivateWorkingSetSize` / `HighAppLatency!`** | OS resource telemetry in the log: free disk space (`FreeDisk:`), the page file (`PageFile:`), RAM consumption (`UseMem:` / `PrivateWorkingSetSize`) and a warning about the main rendering thread hanging (`HighAppLatency!`). |
| **`OnReadEvent: <EIdReadTimeout/EIdReadTimeout>`** | A TCP socket read timeout of the Indy library on connection loss with the proxy or server. |
| **`Looks like order was done while replacing or rest quantity too low`** | During a re-placement (`Replace`) the order managed to fill completely on the exchange, or its unfilled remainder (after hitting someone else's fractional order without BNB in the order book) became smaller than the exchange minimum lot `MinLot` / `MinNotional`. The terminal closes such an order in the interface as normal. |
| **`CanceledToJoin`** | The old closing `Sell` order was cancelled by the terminal as normal to be merged with a newly filled leg of the position by `JoinSellKey` / `MultiOrders` (this frees a `MaxOrdersPerMarket` slot on the first strategy). |
| **`EMAFilter: ...`** | A log entry (and the trade comment in `Reports`) with the computed values of all `CustomEMA` formulas (including the `> -888` collector thresholds) at the moment of the real purchase. |
| **`AutoStart: FALSE <Filter reason>`** | A refusal of auto-buy on a signal (or a repeat shot `MShotRepeatAfterBuy` / `HookRepeatIfProfit` not being placed). **A 3-step investigation algorithm:** 1) Find in `logs/LOG_YYYY-MM-DD.log` the exact second when the repeat order should have been placed; 2) Find the `Signal` line with the `AutoStart: FALSE` marker; 3) Read the exact text of the filter or penalty that fired right after `AutoStart: FALSE`. |
| **`Some detection calls are missed... Reduce strategies count or use more powerful VPS`** | The main thread fell behind the detect computation thread by more than 10 cycles in a row because of too many resource-heavy strategies (`EMA`/`MoonHook`/`Spread`) or insufficient CPU performance on the VPS. |
| **`--- Fill Timing ---` and `Avg wasted cancel` (`Log Analyzer`)** | A diagnostic block of the log analyzer (`2026`): `Fill Timing` shows the distribution of delays between placement, `Replace` and `Fill`, and `Avg wasted cancel` — the average time of late cancellations that reached the exchange after the order had actually filled. |
| **`Uuups! looks like I'm stuck...`** | The main thread or a network worker did not respond for longer than the watchdog timer threshold (usually when free RAM runs out `< 30–50 MB` on a weak 1 GB VPS or at a 100% disk/CPU freeze). |
| **`<-- Splitted tag 0`** | The position was split (`Split`) via the menu at a moment when the terminal's main `START` button was off — such an order loses its binding to the tracking worker! Always keep the `START` button pressed. |
| **`Stop level (%s) is below than liq level (%s); will replace to liq level only`** and **`StopAboveLiq Level changed`** | The protection against exiting below the liquidation price fired: the computed price of a `Panic Sell` step or a stop crossed the liquidation price, and the terminal limited it to the liquidation level (or an offset from it). `DontSellBelowLiq = YES` acts only on Isolated margin with leverage `> 1`; `StopAboveLiq > 0` works from the available liquidation price in any margin mode and takes part in the main stop and `PriceDown`. ## 3. Order statuses in the interface and what to do when an order hangs

* **Status `(5)` / `Replacing` on a `Sell` order:**
  * Means that the terminal sent a request to re-place or cancel the order, but the response from the exchange has not been received yet (or broke off with error `12030`), and the terminal is now looking for the order's actual fate in the exchange trade history.
  * **What you must NEVER do in `One-Way Mode` at status `(5)`:** Do not repeatedly hit the Sell button in the Balances window in panic or close the position by hand on the exchange website! If your manual sale closes the long, and a second later the terminal's queued `Sell` order reaches the exchange, it will sell **a second time** and open a naked `Short` in a rising market!
  * **The correct solution:**
    1. `Hedge Mode` protects against an accidental flip: the closing order is bound to the position side (not via `reduceOnly` — by specifying the side). The position mode is an account property. On Binance Futures it must be **the same for the working bots and the control bot**: there the control bot places the closing order according to its own config's mode, and on a mismatch the exchange rejects its closing order. On Bybit and Gate the terminal itself switches the account to one-way mode.
    2. On modern versions, enable **atomic `Modify`** (the `Ignore the order replacing errors` checkbox in `Settings -> Advanced`), which on Binance Futures and Hyperliquid re-places the order with one atomic price-change command, without a separate cancel and new placement (paired with the stuck-position control on one bot); on Hyperliquid, after such a re-placement the order has a new exchange ID.
* **`ByBit error: N-1002 []` (`LOG Archive`) errors, `Last Error: error in FastParsePositionInfo`, the BGB price limit on Bitget (`buy-in price cannot be higher than`), the shutdown of conditional orders on `ws-fapi.binance.com` (`order.place (ws-fapi)`) and the minimum lot step log `Min piece: 100.00000000`:**
  * **`ByBit error: N-1002 []`** (`2024_Q4`): means that the Bybit server (or an intermediate Cloudflare gateway) returned a non-JSON response to an HTTP request (an empty string or an HTML error page), the full dump of which the terminal saves to the **`LOG Archive`** folder.
  * The order log line **`Order size 2000.00$ Min piece: 100.00000000`** on Bybit (`2022_Q4`, a line from old versions) is not an error but the normal output of the minimum lot step (`lot step`) in coins for cheap shitcoins (where the minimum step equals 100 coins).
  * The position parsing error at startup **`Last Error: error in FastParsePositionInfo`** on old builds occurred when the format of empty fields in the exchange response changed and was eliminated in the `Moonbot-M6` update.
  * On Bitget Spot, when auto-buying the `BGB` token to pay fees during a sharp price move, the exchange may return a price deviation limit error **`{"code":"41118","msg":"BGB/USDT buy-in price cannot be higher than 3.606"}`** (`buy-in price cannot be higher than`, `2026_Q1`).
